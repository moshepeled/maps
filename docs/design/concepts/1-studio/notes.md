# Concept 1 — Studio (pro GIS workstation) · rev 2

- **Idea.** Snapland as a precision instrument (a DAW or Mapbox Studio feel). The chrome recedes, colour comes only from the imagery and the shapes, and every value is set in tabular mono. Panels are docked so nothing floats over the work: title bar 44 · tool rail 52 · options bar 44 (the docked HUD) · map · inspector 352 (Selection, History, People, Activity) · status bar 26. The status bar shows the C-27 readout (`32.077350, 34.783880 · ITM E 179749.0 N 664973.0`), zoom, a live scale bar, the C-26 summary and context key hints.
- **Frames** (`mockup.html#theme=…&base=…&frame=…&input=…`): `desktop.png` dark · Aerial · mouse drawing (shared scenario) · `mobile.png` touch Drawing only · `mobile-selected.png` phone AreaSelected (peek sheet + own toast) · `desktop-map-keyboard.png` dark-tinted OSM, map focus ring + C-06.9 reticle, readout = map centre · `desktop-light-1024.png` light chrome · OSM · inspector as overlay (< 1200 px) · own toast bottom-centre.
- **Chrome.** Dark: bg `#0a0c0f / #111418 / #181c22 / #20252d`, text `#e8ebf0 / #a9b2bf / #8b95a4`, input border `#5c6676`. Light ("paper"): bg `#e9ecf0 / #f6f7f9 / #ffffff / #e3e7ec`, text `#12161c / #434c59 / #58616e`. Accent = *me* (my draft, selection, primary): dark `#22d3ee` (text `#67e8f9`, ink `#03161a`), light `#0e7490` (text `#0b6478`, white ink). Live `#34d399`, danger `#f87171` (light `#dc2626`).
- **Collaborators (12 neon colours, rebuilt; ink `#0a0c0f`).** You lime `#b4f500` · Dana orchid `#f461ff` · Omer tangerine `#ffab61` · copper `#b86e3d` · mint `#bcfab2` · violet `#a64ef4` · periwinkle `#9888d7` · pink `#ff8fcb` · gold `#f6dd79` · cobalt `#447ec1` · plum `#c44f9d` · grass `#5eae29`. Checked with contrast-check.mjs's CIEDE2000 + Machado CVD code. Minimum normal ΔE is 14.9 (gate 14); deutan 6.6 and protan 6.3 (gate 6); accent vs palette 29.4 normal and 7.9 CVD; danger 20.1; saved white 25.1. The old set failed: gold/amber 10.4, violet/lavender 11.7, violet/periwinkle 7.9, orchid/violet protan 0.4. Pink/blush passed at 15.0.
- **"Me" reads as one colour.** My avatar keeps my presence colour (what others see) but gets a 1.5 px cyan ring and a cyan status badge. The People row has a cyan "You" tag, my history nodes are cyan, and my draft and selection are cyan.
- **Presence.** Avatars 28 px, 10 px gaps, 16 px badges that stick out 6 px: plus on the person's colour = drawing, grey eye = viewing (UI.md shows no badge for viewing; this concept adds a quiet one). On phones this collapses to a count button (`3`, "3 on the map").
- **Map styling.** Aerial is dimmed about 26% and never inverted. In the dark chrome, Map is OSM tinted (invert + hue-rotate, desaturated). On dark maps, overlays have a bright core on a `#05070a` casing at 75%. On the light OSM map, my shapes and saved shapes use a dark core (`#0e7490` / `#2f3a4a`) on a white casing, and collaborator neon keeps a 62% dark casing. Selection: accent core, glow, CAD corner brackets. My mouse draft: solid line, dashed rubber-band, dotted closing preview. Touch: placed points shown closed, no cursor. Others' drafts: dashed, dots, chip "Omer · drawing · 0.46 km²" (no km² below 600 px).
- **Toasts.** One bottom-centre lane (phone: above the control row), with a 2 px Undo countdown. Collaboration toasts are held while I draw, and the Activity header says "Toasts held while you draw" (§6.5). The scenario's "Dana saved Yarkon Park Plot · 0.84 km²" is an Activity item at 11:20 (= v2), which fits v3 = my rename at 11:58.
- **Typography.** IBM Plex Sans 13/18 UI, 18/24 600 titles, 11 px caps micro-labels (+0.08em). IBM Plex Mono for every value. IBM Plex Sans Hebrew for Hebrew attribution and names. No text is smaller than 11 px (checked in the DOM). Rail key hints are now in tooltips and the status bar; Measure/M and V were removed.
- **Phone (390, checked at 360).** Title 48 · HUD 58 (readout + one line "Tap the first point or Finish.") · map 666 (90.8% free, gate 45%) · bar 72 `Cancel ······ Undo · Finish`. AreaSelected: peek sheet `max(176px, 30dvh)` covers the bar, Delete has a text label. All targets are 44 px or more; one-tap layer toggle; 2-line Aerial attribution.
- **Why it fits.** Collaborative GIS is instrument work. Docked panels keep chrome off the map; one inspector holds selection, history, people and activity. The status bar always shows WGS84 and ITM, scale and key hints for keyboard-first Rina. Dark chrome gives the most contrast next to imagery for Noa.
- **Pros.** Imagery stays legible; numbers scan like instruments; dense but calm; light and dark share one token contract; the palette now passes the project's CVD gates.
- **Cons / risks.** Docking costs about 404 px of width, so the inspector becomes an overlay below 1200 px (People/Activity collapse there). The tinted OSM changes brand colours such as parks and highways. The neon palette differs from tokens.css `--collab-*`, which the SPEC test parses, so adopting it means changing that single source. `desktop.png` is still a composite (Drawing plus an open selection) because the shared scenario asks for it; the phone and extra frames keep modes exclusive (§3.4). Aerial shows Esri tiles as a stand-in, with the GovMap + Esri credit only.
