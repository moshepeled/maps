# Concept 4: Multiplayer canvas ("Figma for maps"), rev 2

**Idea.** The map is a shared canvas, and people carry the colour. The chrome works like a design tool: tools centred in the top bar, a docked People / Areas / Activity sidebar, a properties inspector on the right, and edge pills for teammates outside the view. All other chrome is neutral grey, so collaborator colours stand out and blue always means "me".

**Frames.** `desktop.png` is the full concept, a composite of the Drawing HUD and the open inspector (in the app these are exclusive modes, UX §3.4). `desktop-core.png` (`#core`) is the [M]-only Drawing frame, with the Areas tab and a keyboard focus ring plus centre reticle (C-06.9). `mobile.png` is phone Drawing. `mobile-selected.png` (`#selected`) is phone AreaSelected: sheet, collaboration toast and edge pills. Elements with `class="n"` are [N]; `#core` hides all of them.

**Palette.** Ink `#1E1E1E`, ink-2 `#5C5C5C`, ink-3 `#8C8C8C` (icons and rings only), line `#E6E6E6`, field `#F5F5F5`, panel `#FFFFFF`. Snap Blue `#1757F5` on tint `#EDF2FF` marks my draft, my selection and primary buttons. Live `#0A7A3B` on `#E7F6EC`, danger `#C8262F`, unread and New badges in ink. People: Dana `#D02670`, Omer `#C2410C`, Moshe `#0E7C73`. The full set of 12 (no blues): `#D02670 #C2410C #0E7C73 #7C3AED #15803D #A16207 #0E7490 #A21CAF #4D7C0F #92400E #BE123C #57534E`.

**Type.** Geist 400/500/600/700 with tabular numerals; Geist Mono for coordinates and key hints. Sizes 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 18 / 22 / 28 px.

**Map.** OSM tiles go through `saturate(.45)` plus an SVG tone curve (.10 / .44 / .76 / .91 / 1). It lifts mid-greys, so OSM's dashed rail and tunnel lines no longer read like drafts, while dark map labels stay legible. *Saved areas:* ink `#2B2F36` outline at 10 % fill, labelled with an opaque white chip "Name · 0.12 km²" at zoom ≥ 14 when the area is ≥ 80 px on screen (C-08); on the phone Rabin Square is 38 px, so it has no label. *Selected area:* 2.5 px blue outline, 6.5 px white casing, **no fill**, and a blue tag "Yarkon Park Plot · 0.84 km²". That separates it from my draft, which has a blue fill, point handles, a dashed rubber-band and a dotted closing edge. *Other people's drafts:* dashed in the author's colour, with the spec chip "Omer · drawing · 0.46 km²" ("Omer · drawing" below 600 px) and the dotted remote rubber-band [M]. *Cursors:* the arrow is [N], off by default (user-menu toggle). Only people who are drawing or editing have one, so Dana has none. When the arrow is on, the draft chip rides on it.

**Desktop layout.** 48 px top bar: brand and Live on the left, then Select / Draw area and Map / Aerial, then presence with **"You" first**, Omer, Dana, then shortcuts and the menu. 300 px sidebar, **People tab by default** (the [M] presence list), with one-shot **Show on map** [N] instead of Follow. The HUD hangs from the top bar. 320 px inspector: Measurements, Details, Description, History, then Edit shape / Zoom / Delete. Edge tabs collapse either side panel. At ≤ 1280 px the inspector floats over the map and the HUD centres and wraps in the free area.

**Phone layout.** 56 px top bar: mark, Live, tools, a presence **count button** and the menu. The HUD has a readout row and a 1-line strip; the control row sits above the bottom bar (Cancel · Undo · Finish). **Every target is 44 × 44 px or larger** (all measured). *Drawing frame:* z14, so my draft, Omer's draft and Yarkon are all in view; the westmost point sits 45 px from the edge. No toast (held while drawing, §6.5) and no sheet. *Edge pills:* 44 px, in a reserved lane the fit keeps free of the working shape: the top or bottom band for targets above or below, compact side bands for targets level with the view.

**Why it fits.** Real-time collaboration is what sets Snapland apart, and here "who is doing what, and where" is the first thing you read. The docked, predictable frame suits long digitising sessions (the Noa and Rina personas). The core still works with every [N] feature off (`desktop-core.png`).

**Pros.** Strongest awareness of the four concepts. Numbers have a stable home in the inspector. Anyone who has used Figma or Miro already knows the model. Neutral chrome keeps any collaborator colour legible, and the calm tiles make the overlays pop.

**Cons / risks.** Docked panels leave an 820 px map at 1440 px, which is why the panels collapse and the inspector floats at ≤ 1280 px. The Activity feed, cursor arrows, edge pills, Show on map and the "last change" line under a person are all [N]. No spec gaps are needed: the Follow gap is gone, and the viewer edge pill uses the existing `PresenceDto.viewport`. Delete assumes Moshe is an admin (C-11). The tone curve should be a setting for people who rely on OSM detail.
