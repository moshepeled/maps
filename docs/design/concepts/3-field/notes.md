# Concept 3: Field (mobile-first, outdoor) · rev 2 after the UX review

**Idea.** A sunlight-readable tool for field crews (persona Avi): ink-on-paper chrome, one safety-yellow signal for "me / go", huge tabular numerals and touch targets ≥ 48 px. The OSM base is 60 % desaturated (`grayscale(.6) contrast(1.05)`), so parks and water keep a hint of colour while overlays own the saturation. Rule: every coloured element has an ink outline, and text on colour is always ink.

**Layout.** The map fills the screen. The black strip (56 px desktop, 60 px phone) holds the mark, LIVE pill, *Areas · 2 in view · 0.96 km²* (`A`, opens C-10), the two-line WGS84/ITM readout (C-27), **presence avatars with status badges** (me first, 10 px gaps, "On map · 3 people"), MAP/AERIAL and the account menu, so awareness survives a closed sheet. DRAW is a round toggle (88 px, `D`); while drawing it is sunk, has an ink body and shows an "On" lamp (`aria-pressed`). One bottom sheet: its black header is the mode console, `Cancel ········ Undo · Finish` (Cancel is a ghost, set apart per C-06.10), and its body is Area · History · On the map. **Toasts sit bottom-centre, 12 px above the sheet.** Phone: control row (zoom) above the sheet, the toast above that, and the DRAW button hidden while drawing. The sheet has tabs **Area · Areas (2) · People (avatars + badges)**; the Area peek is one row (name · km² · expand).

**Map styling.** Saved: ink line on a white halo, 10 % ink fill. **Selected: yellow band outside the edge only (SVG mask) with an ink rim, and an ink double line on the edge. No fill, plus an ink "✓ SELECTED" tag.** **My draft: yellow line in an ink casing, sparse hatch (2.6 px yellow + 0.8 px ink edge every 18 px ≈ 19 %).** Square handles, the first point a yellow target, the last point filled ink. With a mouse, a hazard-dash rubber band runs to the cursor. On touch there are only placed points and a dotted closing edge, with no crosshair. Others' drafts are a dashed colour over a dashed ink casing, with chip `Omer · drawing · 0.46 km²` (phone: `Omer · drawing`).

**Palette.** Ink `#0B0B0C`, paper `#FFFFFF`/`#F3F3EF`, secondary `#4A4C52`, signal `#FFD400`, live `#1ED760`, warn `#FF9A3C`, offline `#FF6B6B`, danger `#C8102E`, rules `#E7E7E1`. Collaborators (ink text ≥ 6.3:1): Dana `#FF4FA3`, Omer `#1FA8FF`, `#FF7A1A` `#14D1C0` `#CCFFBB` `#FFBBFF` `#FF55DD` `#22AAAA` `#DD9977` `#44FFDD` `#9988CC` `#FF99AA`.

**CVD / ΔE00 check (Machado 2009 simulation).** The old lime `#B5E61D` vs signal yellow scored 19.6 in normal vision, but only 1.5 (protan) and 2.7 (deutan), so it collapsed into "me" and is replaced. Coral `#FF5C5C` vs danger passed (≥ 14.4) but read as "error", so it is replaced by `#FF99AA` (≥ 27.8). Mint vs teal was 6.6 in tritan; it is now `#44FFDD` vs `#14D1C0` at ≥ 9.1. Omer vs violet was 1.0 in deutan; violet is now `#9988CC`. Every collaborator is now ≥ 11.3 from yellow and ≥ 17 from danger in all four views. Palette-wide minimum: normal 10.3, protan 5.4, deutan 6.3, tritan 4.5 (was 1.0). No 12-colour set separates fully under dichromacy, so initials and names still carry identity (§6.2).

**Typography.** Barlow 500–800 (highway-signage roots, min 15 px/600 on the map UI), and Barlow Condensed 800 for numerals (68/50 px readouts, `tabular-nums`). Field density uses 12–14 px caps at +0.08–0.12 em for labels. **Office density** (`?density=office` → `desktop-office.png`) is for desk planners: sentence case, 0 tracking, a 36 px readout, 50 px area figure, 44 px targets and a 68 px DRAW button.

**Why it fits.** Area numbers are the product, so they are the largest thing on screen. The readout, Finish and Undo sit in the phone's thumb zone. Colour is reserved for people, so presence reads at a glance even in glare, from the strip or the People tab.

**Pros.** Legible on OSM and aerial because every overlay is ink-cased. Selected, my draft and others' drafts differ in line structure (double line / cased line / dashed), fill (none / hatch / tint) and marker (tag / handles / dots), not only in colour. One sheet surface gives a simple responsive model. Hard 3–5 px ink "lifts" instead of blur.

**Cons / risks.** Still loud for an all-day desk session; Office density mitigates this. The dock costs about 250 px of desktop map height. The yellow band and the draft line are both yellow, told apart by structure. Phone at z14 is dense once the toast and control row are shown. Pastel collaborators (`#CCFFBB`, `#FFBBFF`) need their ink casing on light tiles.

**Scenario.** This is a composite: in the app, Drawing and an open area panel never co-exist (UX §3.4), and §6.5 holds collaboration toasts during Drawing (flushed as one summary afterwards). The toast is shown here to fix its slot. Mouse: 5 points plus the cursor = 1.27 km² (4.28 km). Touch has no provisional point, so the phone's 5 placed points (5th at the desktop cursor spot) close to 1.27 km² on their own. The Rabin label is hidden on phone (< 80 px, C-08).
