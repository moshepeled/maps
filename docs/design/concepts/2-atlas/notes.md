# Concept 2: Atlas (editorial cartography), revision 2

**Idea.** A printed atlas you can draw in. The map is full-bleed under warm paper: OSM tiles get a sepia tint plus a 30% paper veil, but only while `data-base-layer=map` (Aerial is never tinted). All chrome is floating paper cards. Saved areas look like engraved plates: hatched fill, an ink rule and serif-italic *cartouche* labels. The selected area gets a cased "double rule" and a leader line to its card. The Areas drawer is a gazetteer with dotted leaders to each figure.

**Palette.** Paper `#FBF7EF` `#F4EDE0` `#ECE3D2`; hairline `#DDD2BF`; control edge `#8C7F6B`; ink `#1F1A14` `#574D40` `#6F6454`; map linework `#2A241D`. **Burnt sienna `#8A3C0B` = me** (draft, Drawing toggle, finish target). Ink = saved data and the primary button. Forest `#2E6B4A` = Live / Current. **Crimson lake `#8F1D3A` = danger**, 38° of hue away from "me"; Delete is always icon + label. Inks: Dana `#1F5A8A`, Omer `#7B2F6E`, Moshe `#8C5A0C`. **Night atlas** (`html.night`): paper `#1E1A15`, ink `#F3ECDF`, me `#EE955F`, danger `#F29AAC`, inks `#8CBDEB` `#DBA3D1` `#E2B25E` with dark text, OSM inverted to umber.

**Type.** Fraunces (variable, opsz): wordmark, names, figures, cartouches, history; lining tabular numerals. Instrument Sans: UI text, 12 px minimum; eyebrows are 12 px caps at +0.09em.

**Desktop (1440).** 20 px gutters. **Phone (390).** Top bar: mark + Live, then presence **count button** (3 colour dots + "3") and menu. Full-width HUD: readout row and a **full-width one-line strip** (≈ 330 px). 48 px *Areas* edge tab. Toasts sit **above the control row** `[Aerial] [scale · attribution] [+ −]`. Bottom bar in Drawing: `× Cancel ······ ↶ Undo · ✓ Finish` (text labels, 44 px high). AreaSelected: the sheet at peek covers the bottom bar. The map auto-fits the free strip.

**Frames** (`mockup.html?f=…&base=aerial&theme=night`): `desktop.png` Drawing · `mobile.png` Drawing (touch: placed points only, closed; no cursor, no card) · `mobile-error.png` refused tap: *Edges can’t cross here.*, dash-dot crimson "warning tape" edges + × marker, one own toast · `mobile-selected.png` sheet + collaboration toast · `desktop-browse.png` *Draw area · D*, presence popover (Omer's draft lives here, not in the Areas list, C-07), toast, pulse in Dana's ink · `desktop-focus.png` map focus ring + surveyor reticle at the centre · `desktop-aerial.png` · `desktop-night.png`.

**Map styling.** My draft: 3.5 px core on a 10 px paper casing, 17% fill preview (C-06.3), 3 px dashed rubber band, dotted closing preview, ringed first point, filled last point. Others' drafts are dashed in their ink on a 6.5 px casing with point dots. On Aerial the hatch turns to paper (62%) and cartouches stay paper chips.

**Why it fits.** Low-saturation paper leaves the most contrast headroom for the live me/Dana/Omer inks. Hatch vs flat tint separates saved from in progress without relying on colour. Serif figures make km² read as report-grade numbers. All UX.md functions are unchanged, and after revision 2 the layout also follows C-05/C-18 as written: no UX.md change is needed.

**Pros.** Distinctive and calm. Labels read without heavy chips. Pen actions sit at the thumb on phones. The drawer uses the dead sea space on Tel Aviv views. The night variant keeps the identity.

**Cons / risks.** The tile tint and veil need a per-layer switch and re-checks on other OSM styles. The veil also mutes basemap context. SVG hatching is costly: at 20k areas use canvas pattern fills. Two web fonts (about 90 KB). The serif can feel "print" in dense lists. The Activity feed is [N] (session-only).

**Scenario caveats.** On desktop, Drawing with the card open is the scenario composite; in the app the modes are exclusive (phone frames and the focus frame show the true states). The collaboration toast is **held** while drawing (§6.5), so in the Drawing frames the event appears as an Activity entry stamped 11:05 (= v2, Dana's reshape; feed rows show the area's *current* name, so it no longer contradicts v3 *Renamed by You*). The toast frames drop "just now". *Delete* is shown because Moshe is an admin. Geometry (GeographicLib): plot 0.844 km² / 3.91 km, Rabin 0.121, my draft with cursor 1.267 (touch, placed only: 1.270), Omer 0.459. C-27 values come from the repo's EPSG:2039 definition.
